Explore Incredible Destinations

India is home to countless beautiful tourist destinations. Explore historical monuments, sacred places, peaceful hill stations and amazing natural wonders.

  • Popular Destinations: Taj Mahal, Goa, Dal Lake
  • Historical Places: Red Fort, Ajanta Caves, Hawa Mahal
  • Sacred Places: Varanasi, Golden Temple, Kedarnath
  • Hill Stations: Manali, Munnar, Ooty
  • Natural Wonders: Meghalaya, Valley of Flowers, Rann of Kutch

Discover Indian Culture and Traditions

India is famous for its colourful culture, traditional festivals, regional food, music, dance and customs. Every state has its own identity and traditions.

Indian Festivals

  • Diwali – Festival of Lights
  • Holi – Festival of Colours
  • Ganesh Chaturthi – Maharashtra
  • Durga Puja – West Bengal
  • Pongal – Tamil Nadu

Traditional Indian Food

  • Vada Pav – Maharashtra
  • Dosa – Tamil Nadu
  • Dhokla – Gujarat
  • Dal Baati Churma – Rajasthan
  • Butter Chicken – Punjab
